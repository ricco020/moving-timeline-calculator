Moving timeline calculator

Moving checklists count down from 8 weeks, but most people find out later than that. Enter your moving day and the day you start: every task gets a real date, the list is squeezed in the same order when you have less time, and the tasks that are already tight are flagged so you do them first. Add your lease notice period to get the date your landlord needs your letter.

Calendar file: one all-day event per task on its date, works with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar and Outlook (import). Nothing is sent anywhere, the page runs in your browser.

How the dates are set

Each task has two lead times: the usual one (how long before moving day people typically do it, for example 50 days to book movers) and the latest sensible one (for example 2 days to file the USPS change of address). These are common planning conventions, not survey data: in summer, around month ends and in big cities, movers and internet installs book out sooner.

Squeeze (default): when you have less than 8 weeks, every usual lead time is scaled down by the same ratio, so the order stays the same, but no task is moved later than its latest sensible date. Keep ideal dates: tasks whose usual date has passed all go on your start date and are marked late.

The lease notice is never squeezed: it is a term of your lease. If the notice date is already past, the calculator says until when you may owe rent. After moving day, the driver license and registration line is a reminder only: the legal deadline depends on your state, check it with your DMV.
